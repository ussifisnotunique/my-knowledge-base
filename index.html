What Are Order Ahead Orders?

Order Ahead items are products with a future fulfillment date. They are prepared in advance and often require a lead time or cutoff. Order Ahead orders require a minimum 72-hour advance order. Examples include:

  • Fruit platter
  • Meat & Cheese platter
  • Chicken Salad Sandwich platter
  • Artisan Sandwich platter
  • Shrimp Cocktail platter
  • Custom Cake
  • Variety Cookie Tray

How Order Ahead orders differ from regular grocery orders

  1. Customers must order before a cutoff time shown on the product page.
  2. Some items, like packs and meal bundles, are sold as a single bundle. Individual components cannot be swapped or refunded separately.
  3. Payment is validated at checkout and the full amount is charged closer to the fulfillment date.

Customer Has Questions About How to Order

Explain how Order Ahead ordering works

  1. The customer selects their choices and fulfillment window before adding the item to their cart.
  2. The lead time appears on the product page. The customer must place the order before the cutoff.
  3. Some items, like packs and meal bundles, include fixed components that are already part of the bundle and cannot be changed.
  4. Some configurable sections let the customer choose the same option more than once, such as two of the same side dish.

Tell the customer

“Order Ahead items are prepared in advance. You’ll select your choices and fulfillment window before adding the item to your cart, and the lead time is shown on the product page.”

Canceling an Order Ahead Item

Modifying and rescheduling are not available for order ahead items. If a customer needs to make changes, they are able to cancel the order up until midnight the day the order was placed and place a new order with the changes.

Order delayed due to severe weather
During severe weather events, order delays may occur due to store capacity limitations, increased demand, and safety-related operational constraints.
Tell the customer
Instacart is aware that severe weather in their area is impacting order delivery times. Instacart and our retail partners are working as safely and quickly as possible to fulfill orders. Apologize sincerely for the delay and thank them for their patience.
If the customer asks for more detail
Severe weather can affect store operations, delivery routing, and overall order volumes in ways that are difficult to predict. Instacart is doing everything possible to complete their order as soon as it is safe to do so.
Offer a clear next step
  • Order still active and within a reasonable window — Advise the customer to continue waiting and monitor their app for updates.
  • Delay is significant or customer wants to cancel — Go to Modify order to cancel and offer a full refund.
▲ DO NOT
  • Speculate on when weather will clear or when delivery will resume.
  • Reference shopper availability, routing tools, or internal staffing.
  • Make promises about compensation unless following an active incident appeasement policy.
